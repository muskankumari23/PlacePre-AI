# PlacePrep AI

## Project Overview

PlacePrep AI is a production-level AI-powered placement preparation platform designed to help students prepare for campus placements from a single platform. Instead of using multiple websites for learning, coding practice, mock interviews, resume building, and job applications, students can access all these services in one place.

The platform combines a Learning Management System (LMS), DSA Practice, Online Quiz System, AI Mock Interview, Resume Analysis, Job Portal, Progress Tracking, and Leaderboard. It provides a structured learning experience that helps students improve their technical skills, monitor their performance, and prepare confidently for placements.

The project is being developed using the MERN Stack with a scalable architecture, secure authentication, and a modern user interface following real-world software engineering practices.


## Problem Statement

Students preparing for placements often need to use multiple platforms for different activities. They use one website for learning courses, another for DSA practice, a different platform for AI mock interviews, another for resume analysis, and separate job portals to apply for opportunities. This makes the preparation process time-consuming, unorganized, and difficult to track.

Many students also struggle to monitor their overall progress because their learning history, coding performance, interview feedback, resume score, and job applications are scattered across different platforms.

PlacePrep AI aims to solve this problem by providing a single, integrated platform where students can learn, practice coding, take quizzes, attend AI mock interviews, analyze their resumes, apply for jobs, and track their complete placement journey in one place.

## Proposed Solution

PlacePrep AI provides a centralized platform where students can access all the essential resources required for placement preparation. Instead of switching between multiple websites, users can learn from courses, practice DSA problems, take quizzes, participate in AI-powered mock interviews, analyze and improve their resumes, apply for jobs, and track their overall progress from a single dashboard.

The platform also provides personalized progress tracking, performance analytics, and leaderboards to help students stay motivated and identify areas for improvement. With secure authentication, a user-friendly interface, and scalable architecture, PlacePrep AI aims to simplify and enhance the entire placement preparation journey.


## Objectives

* To provide a single platform for complete placement preparation.
* To help students improve their technical and interview skills through structured learning.
* To simplify placement preparation by integrating courses, DSA practice, quizzes, AI mock interviews, resume analysis, and job applications in one system.
* To enable students to track their progress and identify areas for improvement using analytics and performance reports.
* To build a scalable, secure, and user-friendly platform using modern web technologies and AI.


The main objective was to create a single platform where students can prepare for placements without switching between multiple websites. The platform helps them learn, practice, track their progress, and improve their overall placement readiness.

## Target Users

### A. Students

Students are the primary users of the platform. They can enroll in courses, practice DSA problems, take quizzes, participate in AI mock interviews, analyze their resumes, apply for jobs, and track their overall placement progress.

### B. Administrators

Administrators manage the entire platform. They can create and manage courses, quizzes, DSA problems, job postings, users, and monitor platform analytics.

### C. Companies (Future Enhancement)

Companies will be able to post job opportunities, review student applications, shortlist candidates, and manage the recruitment process through the platform.


## Core Modules

### 1. User Authentication

Secure user registration, login, logout, and role-based access using JWT authentication.

### 2. Learning Management System (LMS)

Students can access courses, watch video lectures, read notes, complete assignments, and track course progress.

### 3. DSA Practice

Practice coding problems based on different topics and difficulty levels, with progress tracking and performance analysis.

### 4. Online Quiz System

Take topic-wise and placement-based quizzes with instant results, explanations, and score analysis.

### 5. AI Mock Interview

Practice technical and HR interviews with AI-generated questions and receive feedback on performance.

### 6. Resume Analysis

Build or upload a resume, receive AI-based suggestions, and improve resume quality for placements.

### 7. Job Portal

Browse job opportunities, apply for jobs, and track application status from a single dashboard.

### 8. Progress Tracker

Monitor learning progress, coding activity, quiz scores, interview performance, and overall placement readiness.

### 9. Leaderboard

Compare performance with other students based on coding, quizzes, and overall platform activity.

### 10. Admin Panel

Manage users, courses, quizzes, DSA problems, job postings, and platform analytics through a dedicated dashboard.
